# Accessibility Statement

**Last Updated:** July 31, 2026
Thomas Abram, LLC | [legal@abram.network](mailto:legal@abram.network)

---

## Our Commitment

ABRAM is committed to making its website and platform accessible to people with disabilities. We want everyone who works in production to be able to use our tools, and we treat accessibility as an ongoing part of how we build.

---

## Standard We Aim For

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities.

This statement describes our goal and our current efforts. It is not a claim of certification, and no third party has audited or certified our conformance.

---

## What We Do

Accessibility is considered during design and development. In practice, that includes:

- Keyboard navigation for interactive elements
- Labels and descriptive text intended for screen readers
- Attention to color contrast in our interface
- Support for reduced-motion preferences

This is continuing work rather than a finished result. As we ship new features, we review them with these considerations in mind.

---

## Known Limitations

We know we are not there yet. Some areas of the application are still being improved, and parts of the product may not fully meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA today.

ABRAM is a data-dense production management tool, and it is best experienced on a desktop browser. Some views are not well suited to small screens.

---

## Feedback

If you run into an accessibility barrier on our website or in the platform, we want to hear about it. Please email [legal@abram.network](mailto:legal@abram.network) with a description of the problem, the page or screen where it occurred, and the browser or assistive technology you were using. We will do our best to respond and to address the issue.
